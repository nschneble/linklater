# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** July 25, 2026
**Last updated:** July 25, 2026

Linklater (the "Service") is operated by Nick Schneble ("we," "us," or "our").
This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your personal
information, and the rights you have over it. It applies to everyone who uses
the Service, and is posted conspicuously via a link on our homepage.

For the purposes of the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR"), the
data controller is:

> Nick Schneble
> Contact: <linklater@fancyenchiladas.net>

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect only what we need to operate the Service:

### Information you provide directly

- **Email address** — used to create and identify your account and to send you
  transactional email.
- **Password** — stored only as a salted cryptographic hash. We never store or
  transmit your plaintext password.
- **Saved links** — the URLs you save to your account, along with metadata our
  server fetches from those pages (title, description, site name, favicon, and
  preview image) so your links display nicely.
- **Preferences** — display settings such as theme, dark/light mode, custom
  theme colors, and accessibility options (color-vision and dyslexic-font
  settings).
- **Two-factor authentication data** — if you enable two-factor authentication,
  we store your TOTP secret and hashed recovery codes.

### Information from third-party sign-in

- If you sign in with Google, we receive your email address and Google account
  identifier from Google. We do not receive your Google password. Google's
  handling of your data is governed by
  [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### Information collected automatically

- **Cookies and local storage** — used solely to keep you signed in and to
  remember your preferences. We do not use cookies for advertising or
  cross-site tracking.
- **Authentication tokens** — session tokens, refresh tokens, and any personal
  access tokens or bookmarklet tokens you create are stored (hashed where
  possible) to keep your account secure.
- **Server logs** — standard technical data (IP address, browser type,
  timestamps) generated when you use the Service, retained for security and
  debugging.

We do **not** use tracking analytics, advertising networks, or third-party
trackers, and we do **not** display ads.

## 3. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information, and we have not done
so in the preceding 12 months. We do not "share" personal information for
cross-context behavioral advertising as defined by the CCPA.

We disclose personal information only to:

- **Service providers** who process data on our behalf and only as needed to
  operate the Service:
  - **InterServer** — hosts the server and database where your data is stored
    (United States).
  - **GreenGeeks** — provides the email service that delivers our transactional
    email; your email address passes through their mail servers when we send
    you a message.
- **Google**, when you choose to sign in with your Google account.
- **Gravatar (Automattic)** — to display your avatar, your browser requests an
  image from Gravatar using a one-way hash of your email address. Gravatar does
  not receive your plaintext email, but if you have a Gravatar account, the
  hash allows them to recognize you. If you have no Gravatar, a generated
  placeholder pattern is shown. See
  [Automattic's privacy policy](https://automattic.com/privacy/).
- **Legal authorities**, if required by law, court order, or to protect the
  rights, safety, or property of our users or others.
- **A successor operator**, in the unlikely event the Service changes hands, in
  which case this policy continues to apply and you will be notified.

When you save a link, our server fetches that page to collect its title and
preview metadata. This request goes to the site you saved; no information about
you is sent other than the standard technical details of any web request made
by our server.

## 4. Cookies, Local Storage, and Do Not Track

We use first-party cookies and browser local storage strictly for
authentication and preferences. These are essential to the Service; disabling
them will prevent sign-in from working.

Because we do not track users across websites or over time for third parties,
we do not respond differently to browser "Do Not Track" signals — there is no
tracking to disable. We honor opt-out preference signals such as Global Privacy
Control ("GPC") with respect to CCPA rights; since we do not sell or share
personal information, no additional action results from such signals.

## 5. Data Retention

- **Account data** (email, password hash, saved links, preferences, tokens,
  two-factor data): deleted immediately when you delete your account.
- **Backups**: we keep encrypted database backups for disaster recovery,
  retained on a rolling schedule of 7 daily and 4 weekly copies. Deleted data
  ages out of all backups within approximately 35 days.
- **Server logs**: we do not log routine page visits. The little our servers
  do log (startup events and errors) is size-capped, automatically overwritten
  on a rolling basis as new entries arrive, and never archived.
- We may retain limited data longer where required by law or to resolve
  disputes.

## 6. International Data Transfers

Our servers are located in the United States. If you access the Service from
the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, or Switzerland, your data is
transferred to and stored in the United States. This transfer is necessary to
provide the Service you request (GDPR Art. 49(1)(b)), and we protect your data
in transit with TLS encryption and at rest with the security measures described
below.

## 7. Your Rights

### All users (CalOPPA)

You may review, update, or delete the personal information in your account at
any time via your account settings — including changing your email address,
updating preferences, and deleting your account entirely — or by contacting us
at <linklater@fancyenchiladas.net>. If we materially change this Privacy
Policy, we will notify you by email and update the "Last updated" date above.

### European Economic Area, United Kingdom, and Switzerland (GDPR)

You have the right to:

- **Access** the personal data we hold about you (Art. 15)
- **Rectify** inaccurate data (Art. 16)
- **Erase** your data ("right to be forgotten") (Art. 17)
- **Restrict** processing (Art. 18)
- **Data portability** — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable
  format (Art. 20)
- **Object** to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21)
- **Withdraw consent** at any time, where processing is based on consent,
  without affecting prior processing (Art. 7(3))

To exercise these rights, contact <linklater@fancyenchiladas.net>. We will
respond within one month. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with
your local supervisory authority.

### California residents (CCPA/CPRA)

You have the right to:

- **Know** the categories and specific pieces of personal information we
  collect, use, and disclose (described in Sections 1–3). In CCPA terms, we
  collect: _identifiers_ (email, account ID), _internet activity_ (server
  logs, cookies), and _user-provided content_ (saved links and preferences) —
  directly from you, from Google when you use Google sign-in, and
  automatically from your device.
- **Delete** personal information we hold about you.
- **Correct** inaccurate personal information.
- **Opt out of sale or sharing** — not applicable; we do not sell or share
  personal information.
- **Limit use of sensitive personal information** — we use account credentials
  only to provide the Service, and never for inferring characteristics.
- **Non-discrimination** — we will never deny service, charge different
  prices, or degrade quality because you exercised your privacy rights.

Submit requests to <linklater@fancyenchiladas.net>. We will verify your
identity (for example, via your account email) before fulfilling a request,
and respond within 45 days. You may designate an authorized agent to submit
requests on your behalf.

## 8. Security

We protect your information using industry-standard measures, including
encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS), salted password hashing, hashed storage of
authentication tokens and recovery codes, optional two-factor authentication,
and access controls. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; if
we learn of a breach affecting your personal information, we will notify you
and applicable regulators as required by law.

## 9. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed at children under 13 (or the higher minimum age
required in your jurisdiction, up to 16 in parts of the European Economic
Area). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children below
these ages. If we learn we have collected such information, we will delete the
account and associated data promptly. If you believe a child has provided us
personal information, contact <linklater@fancyenchiladas.net>.
